Sign in with your NaviLink account. The plugin reads your appliances from the NaviLink cloud, the same service the NaviLink app uses, and lists whatever it finds below with the accessories each one can actually support.

Your password is sent to Navien to sign in and is stored in the Homebridge configuration file so the plugin can sign in again after a restart. It is never written to the Homebridge log.

Signing in lists your appliances and asks each one what it is fitted with. Nothing is switched on or changed.
